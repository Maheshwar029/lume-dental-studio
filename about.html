Clinical decisions should be explained, not simply handed to you.

Every patient arrives with a different concern, history and comfort level. Our job is to understand the problem first, then explain what we see, what the options are and what each next step involves.

That means a consultation is not just a treatment recommendation. It is a conversation between you and your dentist.
