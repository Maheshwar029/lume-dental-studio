Treatments→Root Canal Treatment

Relieve infection. Preserve your natural tooth.

Root canal treatment removes infected or inflamed tissue from inside a tooth, cleans the root canals and seals them so the natural tooth can be restored and protected.

Carefully planned from diagnosis to restoration.

Your dentist first confirms the source of pain or infection through examination and appropriate imaging. The tooth is then numbed and isolated while the infected tissue is removed and the canals are cleaned, shaped and sealed.

01DiagnoseExamine symptoms, the tooth and supporting structures, using imaging when needed.
02Clean the canalsRemove infected pulp and carefully clean and shape the inside of the tooth.
03Seal & restoreSeal the canals and plan the final restoration needed to protect the tooth.

For teeth with inflammation or infection inside the tooth.

Root canal treatment may be considered when the pulp inside a tooth is inflamed or infected. A dentist needs to assess the tooth and confirm the cause of symptoms before recommending treatment.

  • Diagnosis comes before treatment, including imaging when appropriate.
  • The tooth is numbed and isolated during the procedure.
  • A final restoration may be needed to protect the treated tooth.

We explain each step before we begin.

Local anaesthesia is used to keep treatment comfortable. Your dentist will also explain what to expect afterward and how the tooth will be restored for normal function.

Clear answers before treatment.

Does root canal treatment hurt?

The tooth is treated under local anaesthesia. Your dentist will explain comfort measures and what to expect afterward.

Why might I need a crown or other restoration afterward?

The recommended restoration depends on the tooth and how much structure remains. Your dentist will discuss the appropriate way to protect it.

Should I wait if tooth pain comes and goes?

Recurring dental pain should be assessed rather than assumed to have resolved. Contact the clinic for guidance if symptoms return.
