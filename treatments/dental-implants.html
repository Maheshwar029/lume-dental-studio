Treatments→Dental Implants

A stable, natural-looking way to replace missing teeth.

Dental implants use a biocompatible implant fixture to support a crown, bridge or denture. Treatment is planned around your oral health, bone support, bite and smile.

Discuss implants →

Every implant case begins with a detailed assessment.

Your dentist examines the missing area and surrounding teeth, reviews your medical and dental history and uses digital imaging when appropriate. This helps determine the implant position and the restoration that will sit on top of it.

01Assess & planEvaluate bone, gums, bite and the space available for the replacement tooth.
02Place the implantThe implant fixture is positioned in the planned location using a controlled surgical procedure.
03Restore the smileAfter healing and integration, a custom restoration is fitted and adjusted for function.

Designed for function as well as appearance.

Good home care and regular dental reviews help protect the implant, gums and surrounding teeth. Your dentist will discuss the full treatment timeline before any procedure.

Start with an implant consultation and a clear clinical plan.
