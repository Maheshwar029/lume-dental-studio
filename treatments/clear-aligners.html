Treatments→Clear Aligners

Straighten your smile with a discreet plan.

Clear aligners are a removable orthodontic option that gradually moves teeth using a sequence of custom trays planned around your bite and treatment goals.

Check your options →

Digital planning meets regular clinical review.

Your dentist or orthodontic specialist evaluates alignment, bite and tooth movement. If aligners are suitable, the planned movements are translated into a sequence of trays with scheduled progress checks.

02Plan the movementsDigital records can be used to map the sequence of tooth movements and tray stages.
03Review progressWear your aligners as instructed and return for clinical checks and refinements when needed.

Your treatment is about more than straight teeth.

Your dentist considers your bite, gum health, facial balance and long-term stability before recommending a plan. Not every orthodontic case is suitable for aligners.
