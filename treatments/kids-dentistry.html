Build trust first, then provide care.

The dentist explains the visit in age-appropriate language, checks teeth and gums, and talks with parents about brushing, diet, fluoride and any concerns about development.

02Check & preventExamine teeth and gums and identify early signs of decay, crowding or other concerns.
03Guide the familyExplain home care and recommend preventive or restorative treatment when clinically needed.
