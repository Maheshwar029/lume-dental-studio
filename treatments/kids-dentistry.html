Build trust first, then provide care.

The dentist explains the visit in age-appropriate language, checks teeth and gums, and talks with parents about brushing, diet, fluoride and any concerns about development.

For children at every stage of growing up.

Children's dental visits can support prevention, early assessment and healthy habits. The approach is adapted to age, development, comfort and the family's concerns.

  • Parents can share health history, habits and any specific concerns.
  • The dentist uses age-appropriate language and allows time to settle.
  • Home-care guidance is part of preventive dental care.

Good dental habits start early.

Regular visits give children the chance to become familiar with the dental environment while parents receive practical guidance to protect developing teeth.

Make dental visits easier for the whole family.

What should my child bring to the first visit?

Bring relevant health or dental information and tell the team about any concerns, sensitivities or previous experiences.

What if my child is nervous?

Tell the team in advance. The visit can be paced around the child's comfort, with simple explanations before examination or treatment.

What can parents do at home?

Consistent brushing, sensible dietary habits and regular dental reviews all support healthy developing teeth.
