Treatments→Dental Check-up & Cleaning

Healthy smiles start with regular care.

A dental check-up combines a clinical examination with professional cleaning and preventive guidance. It gives your dentist a chance to spot changes before they become bigger problems.

Book a check-up →

A calm, step-by-step visit.

Our dentist begins by asking about your concerns, dental history and any sensitivity or pain. We then examine your teeth, gums and bite and recommend imaging only when clinically appropriate.

01Listen & examineWe understand your concerns and check your teeth, gums, bite and oral tissues.
02Clean & assessProfessional cleaning removes plaque and calculus, followed by a focused assessment.
03Explain & planYour dentist explains findings and preventive steps or treatment options, if needed.

A useful starting point for ongoing oral health.

A check-up may be appropriate if it has been a while since your last dental visit, you have a new concern, or you want professional preventive care. Your dentist will tailor the visit to your history and current symptoms.

  • Bring any current concerns, medications or previous dental information you have.
  • Expect time for questions before examination and cleaning.
  • If further treatment is recommended, it will be explained before you decide.

Prevention is part of treatment.

Regular examinations can help identify cavities, gum problems, worn teeth and other changes early. Your dentist will recommend a recall schedule based on your individual oral health.

Questions patients often ask.

How often should I have a check-up?

Your dentist can recommend a recall interval based on your oral health, risk factors and previous findings.

Will I always need an X-ray?

Not necessarily. Imaging is recommended when your dentist believes it will help diagnose or plan care.

Can a cleaning replace daily brushing and flossing?

No. Professional cleaning complements consistent home care rather than replacing it.
