Treatments→Smile Makeover

A natural-looking smile, planned around you.

A smile makeover brings together one or more cosmetic treatments when a patient wants to improve the colour, shape, proportion or appearance of their smile.

Discuss your smile →

We design the treatment around your face, teeth and goals.

Your dentist examines tooth structure, gum health, bite and existing restorations. Depending on your needs, the plan may include whitening, bonding, veneers or restorative care.

For patients who want to explore changes to their smile.

A smile makeover can combine cosmetic or restorative treatments, but the right approach depends on tooth structure, gum health, bite and the changes you want to make.

  • Start with a conversation about what you would like to change.
  • Your dentist assesses health and function before cosmetic treatment.
  • Alternatives, limitations and maintenance are discussed before you commit.

Cosmetic dentistry should still look like you.

We focus on proportion, shade, tooth structure and function rather than a one-size-fits-all smile. Your dentist will explain alternatives and limitations before treatment.

Plan the result, not just the appearance.

What treatments can be part of a smile makeover?

Depending on the case, a plan may include options such as whitening, bonding, veneers or restorative treatment. Your dentist will recommend only appropriate options.

Is cosmetic treatment always necessary for a smile concern?

Not always. Your consultation can include preventive, orthodontic or restorative alternatives where they are more appropriate.

Will my result look natural?

Natural appearance depends on the treatment selected, tooth proportions, shade and individual anatomy. Your dentist will discuss realistic options before treatment.
