Patient educationThis guide explains common dental concerns in general terms. It is not a diagnosis and does not replace an examination by a qualified dental professional.

What are crooked teeth?

Crooked teeth can include crowding, spacing, teeth that stick out or a bite in which the upper and lower teeth do not meet normally. Some alignment differences are mainly cosmetic, while others can make cleaning, chewing or tooth wear more difficult.

Why do teeth become crowded or misaligned?

Tooth and jaw development, inherited characteristics, early or late tooth loss, and some childhood habits can contribute to alignment problems. The cause and severity vary from person to person.

What to noticeChanges in pain, swelling, bleeding, sensitivity, function or appearance.
What an assessment addsClinical examination helps separate similar-looking symptoms and identify the right next step.

What can an orthodontic clinician do?

An orthodontic assessment may include an examination of the teeth and jaws, photographs, scans and sometimes X-rays. The clinician considers the bite, available space, gum health and treatment goals before recommending an approach.

What treatments can straighten teeth?

Depending on the case, treatment may involve fixed braces, removable aligners or another orthodontic appliance. Clear aligners can be appropriate for some cases but are not suitable for every type or complexity of tooth movement.

What should you do during orthodontic treatment?

Follow wear and cleaning instructions, keep teeth and gums carefully clean and attend scheduled reviews. Avoid attempting DIY tooth movement or unsupervised orthodontic appliances; professional assessment and monitoring are important for safety.

What happens after teeth are straightened?

Retention is usually an important part of orthodontic care because teeth can move after active treatment. Your clinician will explain the type of retainer and the recommended wearing schedule.

FAQ

Quick answers to common patient questions.

Do I need braces if my teeth are only slightly crooked?

Not necessarily.

Are clear aligners better than braces?

They are different treatment systems.

Can adults straighten their teeth?

Yes.
