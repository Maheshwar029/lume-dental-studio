Common questions.

Talk to our care team →
Can I book an appointment online?+

Yes. Choose your preferred treatment, clinic and time through the appointment form. Our team will confirm the request.

Do you accept walk-ins?+

Walk-ins may be accommodated when a dentist is available, but booking ahead helps reduce waiting time.

How much does treatment cost?+

Costs depend on your diagnosis and treatment plan. We explain the recommended care and final estimate before treatment.

What should I bring to my first visit?+

Bring any previous dental records or scans you have, a list of current medicines if relevant, and your preferred ID/payment details.
