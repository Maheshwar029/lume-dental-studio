Tools that support better conversations.

01

Digital Imaging

Digital dental imaging can help clinicians assess teeth, roots and surrounding structures when an image is clinically indicated.

02

Intraoral Cameras

Small cameras can show areas of the mouth on screen, making it easier for patients to see and discuss what the dentist sees.

03

Digital Treatment Planning

Digital records can support planning for orthodontic, implant and restorative treatments when appropriate.

04

Sterilization Protocols

Instrument processing follows documented cleaning, packaging, sterilization and storage workflows appropriate to clinical care.

05

Patient Education

Images, diagrams and treatment simulations can help turn technical dental information into something easier to understand.

06

Comfort-Focused Care

Modern equipment and careful clinical workflows are selected with patient comfort, efficiency and safety in mind.
